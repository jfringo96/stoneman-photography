Prints

Sizes, papers and prices for prints of my photos.

Below you'll find the sizes, papers and prices for prints. To order one, send me a message using the contact form below or on the Contact page, with the title of the photo, the size and the paper you'd like, and I'll get back to you.

Choose from the sizes below, and use the toggle to switch between landscape and portrait. A print may be cropped slightly differently from the version shown on the site to fit the size you pick. If the difference would be significant, I'll let you know and suggest an aspect ratio that suits the photo better.

There are two papers to choose from. Standard is good-quality photographic paper and is suitable for most prints. Fine Art is closer to the paper used for gallery art prints: higher quality, for special prints you want to display as a centrepiece.
