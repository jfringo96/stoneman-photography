Jake Stoneman

Wildlife & Nature Photographer

Welcome to Stoneman Photography. I'm a wildlife and nature photographer based in the UK, drawn to the quiet moments that happen just before the world notices — a heron holding still at the water's edge, a deer half-hidden in morning mist, the brief window of golden light before it disappears.

I picked up a camera seriously a few years ago and haven't put it down since. What started as early morning walks with a borrowed lens has turned into something that shapes how I move through the world — slower, more patient, always watching.

Most of my work is done in the field, often in the rain, often before dawn. I shoot primarily in the countryside and wetlands around the UK, though I'll travel wherever the wildlife takes me. I use a mirrorless setup and tend to favour natural, available light over intervention — if it doesn't look right in the moment, I'd rather wait than force it.

This site is a place to share that work. If something here resonates with you, or if you're interested in prints, licensing, or just want to talk photography, feel free to get in touch.
